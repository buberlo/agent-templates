## Pitfalls

Skills are workflows/instructions (progressive disclosure), MCP are connections and tools. Combine the two, not confuse them. Sources: https://code.claude.com/docs/en/features-overview , https://github.com/ComposioHQ/awesome-claude-skills
- Activate only the MCPs/Skills that the task needs. Hermes filters per include/exclude; OpenClaw has tool policy and prompt budgets. Sources: https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/features/ends/mc️
- Do not write tokens or passwords in chat, skill files or commands of custom MCPs. Grok bot wiki: OAuth tokens remain on the connector backend; Command servers must not contain credentials in argv. Source: https://www.grokbotwiki.com/guides/grok-bot-plugins-mcp
- write, send, pay, deploy and delete only after explicit approval; good document skills require a Verify step (re-read file, render PDF/PNG).
